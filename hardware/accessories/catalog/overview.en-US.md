The catalog is being prepared. This page does not publish an approved accessory list or establish a production-lifetime commitment.

| Evidence category | Information required before listing |
|---|---|
| InHand offering | Exact supplier/model, official listing and applicable board revisions |
| Tested third-party accessory | Test record, sample/revision, software baseline, conditions and result |
| Community report | Public report and its scope; independent InHand testing must not be implied |

An accessory entry and its board compatibility record are separate. A connector that fits does not establish electrical, software or enclosure compatibility.
