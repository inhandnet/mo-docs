## Ethernet

Gigabit Ethernet with DHCP by default.

## Wireless

The Mo 68A has **no on-board Wi-Fi or Bluetooth**. Use a USB Wi-Fi / Bluetooth adapter. InHand will offer one tested adapter as an official accessory, with its driver pre-installed in the image, plus a list of other verified adapters.
