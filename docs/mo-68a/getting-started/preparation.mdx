## Required items

To set up your Mo68A, prepare the following five items.

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| USB-C power supply | 5 V / 5 A recommended. Any brand meeting the board's power requirements can be used. |
| Computer | Windows, macOS or Linux, to write the SD card |
| microSD card | 32 GB or larger recommended; check the capacity required by the selected Mo68A OS release. Choose a UHS-I card rated Class 10 / U1 or higher. |
| microSD card reader | A built-in slot or USB reader compatible with your card, for writing the OS image |
| Cooling fan | A compatible 4-pin, 5 V PWM fan. Install and connect the fan before powering on the board, and keep it running during use. |

:::warning[Cooling is required]
Mo68A requires a cooling fan. Operating the board without one can cause
overheating and trigger over-temperature protection. The fan is required, not an
optional accessory.
:::

## Connection and access

Choose an access method and prepare the equipment for it. You do not need all
three setups: Ethernet provides network access, debug UART provides a serial
console, and a monitor with a keyboard and mouse provides local desktop access.

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Ethernet | An Ethernet cable and a network connection between your computer and Mo68A, for remote access such as SSH once networking is configured |
| Debug UART | A compatible USB-to-UART adapter cable for the board's debug connector, plus a serial terminal on your computer |
| Local desktop | A mini DisplayPort-to-DisplayPort cable and a DisplayPort monitor, plus a USB keyboard and mouse. A compatible DSI display can be used as an alternative display. |

## Optional accessories

Prepare these accessories only if you need their functions.

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| RTC backup battery | A compatible battery for retaining the real-time clock while the board is powered off |
| CSI camera | A compatible MIPI CSI-2 camera with a suitable ribbon cable for camera and AI examples |


## Choosing a microSD card

Use a **32 GB or larger** microSD card to leave room for system updates,
applications, AI models and your own files. No particular brand is required.

Check the capacity required by the Mo68A OS release you download: image sizes
can change between releases. The card must hold the entire uncompressed image,
not just the downloaded `.xz` or `.zip` file. Card capacities use decimal GB;
image sizes may use binary GiB.

For general use, we recommend a **UHS-I** card rated **Class 10 / U1 or higher**.
UHS-I identifies the bus interface; Class 10 and U1 identify speed classes, not
card capacity. These are selection recommendations, not a guarantee of a
particular transfer speed on Mo68A. See the
[SD Association's speed-class guide](https://www.sdcard.org/developers/sd-standard-overview/speed-class/)
for the markings used on cards.

An **A1-rated** card is a useful choice for an OS and applications because its
rating includes random-access performance. A2 is not required; its additional
performance depends on host support, so an A2 label alone does not guarantee
better performance on Mo68A. See
[Application Performance Class](https://www.sdcard.org/developers/sd-standard-overview/application-performance-class/).

Prepare a compatible microSD card reader for your computer. Back up any files
you need from the card: writing the OS image replaces its partition table and
erases existing data. You do not need to format the card manually before writing
the complete image. Follow [Install the OS](./install-os.mdx) for the installation
steps.
