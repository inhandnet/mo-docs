The Mo 62A is built on the **TI AM62A74** vision processor: four Arm Cortex-A53 cores at 1.4 GHz, a Cortex-R5F real-time core, and a C7x DSP with a Matrix Multiply Accelerator that delivers **2 TOPS** of AI inference. An on-chip ISP with VPAC handles WDR, lens distortion correction and RGB-IR before frames reach your model.

## Mo 62A vs Mo 68A

Mo 62A is the entry board: one camera, lower power, on-board Wi-Fi. For two cameras, 4K60 video, USB 3.0 or PCIe, use the [Mo 68A](../../mo-68a/hardware-development/overview.mdx). Full table: [Compare the boards](/hardware#compare).
