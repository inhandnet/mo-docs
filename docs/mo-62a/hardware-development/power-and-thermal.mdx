## Power supply

| | |
|---|---|
| Input | USB Type-C, 5 V |
| Recommended supply | 5 V / 5 A |
| Maximum consumption | 25 W |
| Operating temperature | 0 – 50 °C |

The 40-pin header's 5 V pins (2 and 4) supply HATs from the same input, so budget for any HAT you add.

## Cooling

A 4-pin 5 V PWM fan header (J6) is on the board. Under sustained AI load, use a heatsink or the fan.
