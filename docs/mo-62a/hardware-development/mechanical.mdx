## Published dimensions

The [Mo62A datasheet (Chinese PDF)](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0369/9536/7051/files/Mo_62A__V1.0_e7e46c7c-d546-41ad-bf9d-0308dbf8e339.pdf) lists an 85 × 56 mm board and a mass of 47 g. Page 3 contains the front, side and connector views reproduced below.

:::warning[Reference only, not a production drawing]
The source explicitly states that all dimensions are approximate, for reference only, and must not be used for manufacturing. Dimensions can change without notice. This page does not replace a revision-controlled mechanical drawing with tolerances.
:::

## Enclosure and cable clearance

![Mo62A board photograph for connector placement reference](/img/boards/mo-62a.png)

Use the photograph to recognise connector locations, not to measure them. A similar board footprint does not guarantee Raspberry Pi enclosure compatibility. Check all of the following on the board revision and accessories you will actually ship:

- micro HDMI, stacked USB and Ethernet plug bodies, cable bends and strain relief.
- USB-C power plug, microSD insertion/removal and access to controls.
- Camera FPC entry, latch access, bend space and correct contact orientation.
- Header/HAT height, underside clearance and safe separation from conductive parts.
- Heatsink, fan, airflow path, antenna and RF-cable clearance.
- Mounting points, insulating spacers and mechanical load from external cables.

See [Hardware interfaces](./hardware-interfaces.mdx) for connector identification and [Power & thermal](./power-and-thermal.mdx) for cooling considerations.

## Production CAD and release status

No verified public STEP model, DXF outline or toleranced production drawing was found in the reviewed Mo62A hardware resources. No case-fit or mounting-load test was performed for this page.

Before enclosure tooling, request the mechanical package for the exact PCB/assembly revision, including connector envelopes, keep-outs, mounting tolerances and approved fasteners. Include the board's silkscreen and ordered SKU; a schematic filename alone is not sufficient revision identification. See [Hardware revisions](./hardware-revisions.mdx).
