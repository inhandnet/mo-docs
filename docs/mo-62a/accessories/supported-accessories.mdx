## Read the validation scope first

This list records evidence from the documented Mo62A setup. An interface being
present is not a qualification of every product with that connector. Where a
manufacturer, part number or revision was not recorded, no model-specific
approval is implied.

| Accessory | Connection | Evidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IMX219 camera with matching ribbon | JP1 CSI, 22-pin / 0.5 mm board connector | Repeated 1920 × 1080 JPEG capture through the TI ISP; not all IMX219 modules or cables |
| Cooling fan fitted to the test board | J1, 5 V PWM, SH1.0-4p | Tachometer and control attributes read; exact fan model and sustained thermal qualification not recorded |
| HDMI display and cable | micro HDMI | Desktop configuration and audio routing documented; no universal display/EDID compatibility claim |
| USB keyboard and mouse | USB 2.0 Type-A | Used in the desktop workflow; exact models not qualified as a product family |
| Wi-Fi antenna | Matching RF plug and antenna specification | Network configuration and association documented; no antenna gain/range qualification |
| RTC backup battery | J2, SH1.0-2p | Connector assignment verified; approved cell type and power-off retention not verified |
| USB-UART adapter | J4 debug UART, 3.3 V TTL | Connector and console settings documented; no new adapter electrical test in this review |
| Header HAT or module | J6, 40-pin | GPIO/UART/PWM loopbacks passed; no complete HAT qualified by those tests |

See [Preparation](../getting-started/preparation.mdx) for power, card and cable
requirements. The published supply rating is 5 V / 5 A; include connected
accessories in the power budget. Do not infer a separate current allowance
from the header's 5 V pins.

## Camera: use the tested combination

Disconnect power before handling the ribbon. Match its pitch, pin count and
contact orientation at both ends; do not assume all Raspberry Pi camera cables
fit the Mo62A connector.

## Fan, battery and debug cables

The published schematics identify J1 as the fan connector, J2 as the battery
connector and J4 as debug UART. The older README uses different fan/header
references. Check the schematic, connector shape and physical pin indication,
not the reference label alone.

| Connector | Pin assignment | Selection check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J1 fan | 1: 5 V; 2: PWM; 3: GND; 4: tachometer | Plug pitch, wiring, voltage and current; never rely only on wire colour |
| J2 battery | 1: GND; 2: VBAT | Obtain the approved battery type and polarity before connecting |
| J4 debug UART | 1: board RX; 2: GND; 3: board TX | 3.3 V TTL; cross TX/RX; no RS-232 voltage or adapter power wire |

## Cases, PoE and expansion products

No case fit, PoE expansion board or complete Raspberry Pi HAT was qualified in
this review. The mechanical drawing is approximate and must not be used as a
production machining drawing. Confirm connector openings, mounting positions,
underside clearance, ribbon paths and airflow with the actual assembly.

Do not infer bare-board PoE support from a schematic block label. Obtain the
specific expansion board's documentation and power-path approval before use.
For header add-ons, continue with [Raspberry Pi HATs](./raspberry-pi-hats.mdx).

When reporting a working accessory, include its manufacturer/part number,
revision, wiring, board revision, image version, overlay, commands and measured
results. Keep “reported by a user” separate from a reproduced test.
