## Evidence status

No verified Mo62A-specific regulatory approval identifier or formal conformity document was available in the material reviewed for this documentation on October 20, 2026. This is a documentation gap, not a finding that the product is uncertified.

The review covered the public [Mo62A documentation directory](https://github.com/inhandnet/Mo62A/tree/master/doc), the published product datasheet and the [official Mo62A product page](https://www.inhand.com/en/products/ai-edge-computers/mo-62a/). These sources describe hardware and software, but they are not substitutes for the applicable approval records or signed declarations.

## Information to confirm for the target market

The topics below are requests for evidence, not a claim that every scheme applies to every configuration or destination.

| Topic | Status in this documentation | Information to request |
|---|---|---|
| United States / FCC | No verified Mo62A-specific identifier or compliance package recorded | Applicable authorization records, responsible party and covered configuration |
| Canada / ISED | No verified identifier or integration package recorded | Applicable records, radio configuration and installation conditions |
| EU conformity | No signed Mo62A declaration available here | Applicable signed declaration, model scope and supporting reports |
| UK conformity | No signed Mo62A declaration available here | Applicable signed declaration and model/market scope |
| Other destinations | Not evaluated here | Destination-specific documentation for the exact product |
| Wireless module | Module identity is described in hardware sources; approval conditions are not verified here | Module identifiers, approved antenna configuration and host integration instructions |

Do not copy a certification number from another InHand product or assume that an on-board radio is pre-certified solely because a module part number is known. No such blanket module-approval claim is made here.

## Match the exact product

The datasheet's order-code page identifies the 2 GB, 4 GB and 8 GB variants. It is reproduced for product identification only, not as evidence of certification.

When contacting InHand through your established support/sales contact or the [official product page](https://www.inhand.com/en/products/ai-edge-computers/mo-62a/), provide the ordered model, PCB/assembly revision, target market, radio/antenna configuration and intended enclosure. Share serial numbers privately when needed.

Use [Declarations & test reports](./declarations.mdx) to check the returned files and [Integration notes](./integration-notes.mdx) to record the final-product configuration. A qualified compliance reviewer should determine the applicable market requirements; this page is an evidence inventory, not a market-access approval.
