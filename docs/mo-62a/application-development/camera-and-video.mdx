## Camera preview (IMX219)

The IMX219 camera works out of the box on the CSI connector. Connect it with the power off: lift the latch, insert the ribbon with the contacts facing the board, and lock the latch.

```bash
sudo imx219-preview.sh          # 15 fps (default)
sudo imx219-preview.sh 30       # 30 fps
sudo GAIN=232 imx219-preview.sh 5   # low light
```

The preview needs the display; the desktop pauses while it runs. Tuning variables:

| Variable | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FPS` | 15 | 5 / 8 / 10 / 15 / 30 | Frame rate (first argument) |
| `WB_R` / `WB_B` | 0.5 / 0.6 | 0.0 – 1.0 | White balance red / blue reference |
| `GAIN` | 150 | 0 – 232 | Analogue gain |
| `DGAIN` | 256 | 256 – 4095 | Digital gain |
| `EXPOSURE` | auto | lines | Exposure |

If the camera is missing, check `v4l2-ctl --list-devices` and `media-ctl -d /dev/media0 --print-topology`.
