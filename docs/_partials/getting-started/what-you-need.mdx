| Item | Notes |
|---|---|

| USB Wi-Fi adapter (optional) | The Mo 68A has no on-board wireless |

</Only>

:::tip[Power supply]
A supply that cannot deliver enough current causes random resets under AI load. Use a 5 V / 5 A USB-C supply.
:::
