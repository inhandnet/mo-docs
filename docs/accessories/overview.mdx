| Level | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Official** | Selected and fully tested by InHand, sold on the official store, with a production lifetime |
| **Verified** | Third-party product that passed the same tests; drivers are in the image. Not sold by InHand |
| **Community** | Reported to work by users; not tested by InHand |
