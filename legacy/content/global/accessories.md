Accessory specifications and usage guides will be maintained here, in one place. Each product's **Accessories** section will link to the applicable guides and list its verified compatibility.

## Library in preparation

No accessory compatibility claims have been published in this site yet. A connector fitting a board does not establish electrical or software compatibility.

Future entries will identify the accessory, supported product and hardware revision, required system image, installation procedure, and test evidence. Official, verified third-party, and community contributions will be distinguished explicitly.
