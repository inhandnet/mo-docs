## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not post suspected vulnerabilities, credentials, private keys, or exploit details in public issues.**

The product security reporting contact and response policy are pending confirmation. Until a dedicated private channel is published, use your existing private InHand support contact to request the appropriate security reporting route. Do not send vulnerability details through an unverified channel.

The repository's [security policy](https://github.com/inhandnet/mo-docs/blob/main/SECURITY.md) is maintained alongside this page. A verified reporting channel is required before this site can be considered ready for the GTM security milestone.

## Advisories and SBOM

Security advisories and version-specific software bills of materials will be linked here when published. The absence of a published advisory does not indicate that a product is free from vulnerabilities.
